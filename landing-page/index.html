The problem

Everyone in the garri supply chain is guessing — alone.

Stores over-order and under-order the same week. Families drive across town chasing a bag of garri. Importers ship blind. Nobody has visibility into what anyone else actually needs.

01 / HOUSEHOLDS

Chasing staples, store to store

Hours spent driving store to store to find garri in stock — then buying an oversized bag "just in case," which just moves the waste home.

02 / STORES & RESTAURANTS

Manual ordering, chronic stockouts

Phone-call reordering with no demand data means top sellers run out while slow movers pile up as dead stock.

03 / SUPPLIERS

Shipping on intuition

Importers and processors have zero downstream visibility, so containers are sized on gut feel, not real demand.

How Oja works

One demand-intelligence layer. Four moving parts.

1

Pantry Profile

Every household, store, and restaurant tells Oja what it actually consumes — once. Not a one-off order, a living profile.

2

AI forecast

Our demand engine predicts what each account will need, weeks ahead — down to the SKU and the region.

3

JIT positioning

Regional cross-docks hold exactly enough buffer stock, restocked on a forecast-driven cadence — not a fixed guess.

4

Fulfillment

Predictable delivery — weekly to stores and restaurants, subscription cadence to households, before anyone runs dry.

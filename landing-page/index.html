FAQ

Questions, answered

What exactly does Oja sell?

Authentic African raw foods — grains and flours (garri, fufu, plantain flour), oils, dried fish and proteins (stockfish, crayfish), spices, fresh produce, and frozen goods. We curate every product and its source; this isn't an open marketplace of resellers.

Is this a subscription? What if I don't want a box that month?

You're always in control. A pantry profile generates a suggested basket on your schedule, and you review, edit, skip, or confirm before anything ships. You can also order one-off with no subscription at all.

I own an African grocery store. Are you competing with me?

No — stores are our core customers and partners. Oja replaces the guesswork of ordering from multiple importers with one reliable weekly delivery at wholesale prices, plus data on what your customers want. Many stores will also serve as neighborhood pickup points.

Where do you deliver?

We're launching metro by metro, starting in Houston, followed by Atlanta, the DC–Maryland–Virginia area, and Toronto. Join the waitlist and we'll open your zone as the network reaches your city.

How do you keep food fresh and authentic?

Just-in-time logistics means we stock micro-warehouses to forecasted demand instead of letting inventory sit for months. Every lot is tracked from supplier to doorstep with expiry-first handling, and perishables carry a freshness guarantee — if it's not right, you're refunded, no return needed.

How is Oja different from ordering on a reseller site?

Resellers buy retail, mark it up 2–3×, and still run out of stock. Oja operates the supply chain itself — from importer relationships to regional warehouses to delivery — with AI demand forecasting keeping the right products in stock at fair prices.

Can my church or association order together?

Yes — group orders are core to Oja. Communities aggregate baskets into one bulk delivery at tiered discounts, perfect for congregations, student associations, and buying clubs.
